Sway and Wayland do figure; Sway provides no less than 19 workspaces (whatever
sway calls them).  Alt + 0, 9 (press and hold Alt, tap 0, release Alt, tap 9)
or in swayspeak iirc that is 'mod' something rather than 'alt'.  Do that to
access workspace 19.  Anything above workspace 9 is accessed that way in this
custom scheme.  Any workspace below workspace 10 is just Alt + <number> so that
Alt + 4 gives workspace 4; and pressing and holding Alt and tapping 5 gives
workspace 5.  No need to let up on the Alt key if stepping through them for
an inspection of their contents.
